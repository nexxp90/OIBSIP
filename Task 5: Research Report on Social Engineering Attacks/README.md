## 📖 What is Social Engineering?

Social Engineering is a cyberattack technique in which attackers manipulate people into revealing confidential information or performing actions that compromise security. Instead of exploiting software vulnerabilities, these attacks target human trust and behavior.

---

## ❓ Why is it Important?

Social engineering remains one of the most common attack methods because it targets people rather than technology. Understanding these attacks helps individuals and organizations recognize suspicious activities and reduce security risks.

## 🔍 Common Types of Social Engineering

### 📧 Phishing

Fraudulent emails or websites designed to steal sensitive information such as usernames, passwords, or banking details.

### 🎣 Spear Phishing

A targeted phishing attack aimed at a specific individual or organization.

### 📱 Smishing

Phishing attacks conducted through SMS or text messages.

### 📞 Vishing

Voice-based attacks where scammers impersonate trusted organizations over phone calls.

### 👤 Pretexting

Creating a fake identity or scenario to convince victims to disclose confidential information.

### 🎁 Baiting

Offering fake rewards such as free software or USB drives to trick users into compromising their systems.

---

## 🛡️ Prevention Techniques

* Verify the identity of unknown contacts.
* Avoid clicking suspicious links or attachments.
* Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
* Keep software updated.
* Attend regular security awareness training.
* Report suspicious emails or messages immediately.
