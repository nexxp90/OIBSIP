## Key Takeaways

During this research, it was observed that social engineering attacks primarily exploit **human behavior** rather than technical vulnerabilities. Common attack methods include phishing, spear phishing, vishing, smishing, pretexting, and baiting. Organizations can significantly reduce the risk of these attacks by implementing security awareness training, multi-factor authentication (MFA), email filtering, strong password policies, and regular cybersecurity assessments.
